01About

From a line on a map to a life’s work.

Growing up in Cameroon, I have lived with the consequences of a line drawn in 1916 by two European diplomats who barely knew the land they divided. That line became my first serious project: The Line, a digital history I research, write, design and code myself.

I studied International Relations at the University of Yaoundé I and work as a freelance web developer, from design to deployment. I enjoy the building as much as the research: maps that draw themselves, timelines you can search, and pages that respect a reader’s data plan.

01The Line

A nine-chapter digital history of Cameroon’s partition, reunification and colonial memory, from German rule in 1884 to the unitary state of 1972, and how Africa and Europe remember it today.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • SVG cartography
  • Archival research
Visit live site ↗
Context
Cameroon’s partition and reunification still shape the country, yet most sources sit in archives, in two colonial languages, out of reach for readers at home.
Role
Founder, researcher, writer, designer and developer: sources, chapters, maps, a zero-dependency Node build and deployment.
Outcome
A live site: 9 referenced chapters, 24 profiles, 50+ timeline events, 50+ licence-checked images, seven archive films and a georeferenced 1949 map.
Lesson
Verify every quotation, credit every image, and present historians’ disagreements fairly.

Exam preparation for students in Cameroon sitting the GCE, the Baccalauréat and IELTS, in English and French, organised by stream.

  • Next.js
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Supabase
  • EN / FR
Visit live site ↗
Context
Students need clear teaching, honest practice and measurable progress, in the language of their exam, on the phone they already own.
Role
Product, brand and full-stack build: the blueprint, the “E as a globe” identity, the Next.js app, content structure and deployment.
Outcome
A live bilingual platform: 109 worked questions, 28 curated videos, timed mock exams, study guides, a revision planner and an IELTS band calculator.
Lesson
Practice is presented as practice, never as an official result. Plain words beat hype.

06Process

A method built on evidence.

  1. 01

    Sources

    I start from the evidence and the real question: archives, readers, users. Nothing is invented.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Content, architecture and technology choices, written down and scoped honestly before design begins.

  3. 03

    Design

    Type, layout and motion as one system, designed for a low-cost phone and two or three languages.

  4. 04

    Build

    Lean, readable code: static where possible, frameworks only where they earn their place.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Every link, source and translation checked; tested on real devices, with reduced motion and slow data.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Deployed, documented and kept improving in the open, not abandoned on launch day.
